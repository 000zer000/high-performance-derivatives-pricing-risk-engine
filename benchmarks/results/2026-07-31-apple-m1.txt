Limitations
- These are elapsed wall-clock measurements from one machine and one run date.
- Five repetitions do not characterize every thermal or system-load condition.
- The Asian serial warm-up/order shows visible variability; medians are reported.
- No cloud-runner or cross-compiler speed claim is made.
- Exact equality applies to these outputs because both execution policies use the
  same path-indexed draws and fixed block-reduction order.
